# Partner co-branding and the Aura sub-brand

A partner mark is a compliance object, not a design element. Nothing here is
style; it is risk control. When in doubt, use the lighter co-branding context
and flag to the user.

## Co-branding contexts, lightest to heaviest

A. Endorsement badge: the partner's own issued badge, used as issued.
B. Built-on text credit (preferred): "Runs on Rockwell Allen-Bradley
   controls with Mondini tray sealing on the line."
C. Ecosystem wall: full-color marks on uniform neutral cards (template
   slide 10).
D. Formal co-brand lockup: only with a written agreement and the partner
   brand team's sign-off. Until then, this stays a template.

## Compliance rules (all decks)

- Never recolor, redraw, trace, crop, or restyle a partner mark. No
  shadow, glow, gradient, outline, skew, rotation.
- Use partner-supplied vector files only. A mark scraped from the web or a
  raster JPEG is not usable in a shipping deck; use the neutral card with a
  text wordmark placeholder and tag [GET].
- Marks sit on white or neutral surfaces only. Never on brand red, never on
  a colored band, never directly on the dark background (put a white card
  under it).
- Honor each partner's own clear space and minimum size; the larger value
  wins.
- Never imply endorsement beyond the agreement. Partners are named as
  partners, not sponsors, unless a sponsorship is real and disclosed.

## The Rockwell red-proximity ruling

Brand red #DC220A and Rockwell red (~#C8102E) read as one color at a
glance. On any slide carrying a Rockwell mark: the mark sits on a neutral
card with at least 1 S of neutral separation from any brand-red element
(rule, CTA, red text); never on the same band as red; never recolor either
red to harmonize.

## Aura and Aura Edge (Option B containment)

Aura is an endorsed sub-brand with its own product language. Decks about
Aura keep the corporate frame and contain the product language:

- Surface: Deep Navy #0C1B33 (template slide 11). Not ink, not white.
- The mark: the open gradient ring (#0078FF to #00E3CC at 135 degrees,
  round caps, hollow center, never filled, never recolored). It is the only
  gradient allowed anywhere in the deck.
- External signature: "Aura by Harpak-ULMA" (Aura Edge: "Aura Edge by
  Harpak-ULMA"). "Harpak-ULMA Aura" only as the in-product identity string.
  "Harpak-ULMA Intelligence" is retired. Only Aura and Aura Edge are named
  publicly; Vision, Flow, Connect, and Aura OS are roadmap, not deck
  content.
- Aura Edge is signaled by one move: EDGE set in the accent color beside
  the shared wordmark. Never a separate symbol. Plain-language definition:
  "Aura Edge runs the same intelligence on hardware at the line, not in
  the cloud."
- Two blues, scope-separated: corporate steel blue #235980 and the Aura
  blue-to-teal never appear on the same surface. On an Aura slide, links
  and info accents use teal #00E3CC, not steel blue.
- Two reds, scope-separated: Aura's brighter #FF3330 exists only inside
  real product screenshots. Deck chrome red stays #DC220A, and they never
  share a surface.
- Type: DM Sans lives inside real product screenshots only. Deck chrome
  around them stays Archivo/Inter/JetBrains Mono.
- Aura product voice on mock panels: action-first, terse, numbered steps,
  a safety callout before any procedure, a confidence tier plus cited
  source on every answer. Example: "Check seal temperature. It should be
  180 to 200 C. SAFETY: full LOTO before opening the panel."

## Legal gates (repeat to the user when relevant)

- Trademark clearance for Aura and Aura Edge sits with counsel; decks
  assert no availability. Aura Edge goes public at Pack Expo 2026 and
  Automation Fair 2026, so clearance is urgent and any public deck using
  the name should note it.
- No formal co-brand lockup may be produced until a partner agreement
  authorizes it.
- The Automation Fair session is co-presented with Rockwell: apply both
  the Aura containment rules and the Rockwell red-proximity ruling on the
  same deck. Signature: "Aura Edge by Harpak-ULMA".
